隐私政策

HashRead 不会收集、存储或分享任何个人信息。

你导入的文档、文件夹书签、生成的记录、偏好设置以及其他内容均保存在你的设备本地。我们无法访问这些数据。

如果你开启 iCloud 同步，数据将存储在你的个人 iCloud 账户中，并受 Apple 隐私政策保护。你可以随时在「设置」中关闭 iCloud 同步。

连接 WebDAV 服务器时，账号密码仅保存在本机钥匙串中，只用于访问你自己配置的服务器，不会上传给我们。

我们不使用任何第三方分析、崩溃报告、追踪或广告服务。

HashRead 不会有意收集儿童的任何信息。应用内不含广告，也不含除反馈邮件之外的外部链接。

我们可能会不时更新本隐私政策。任何变更将在此页面反映。

如果你对隐私有任何疑问或担忧，请通过 kazecreator@gmail.com 联系我们。

Privacy Policy

HashRead does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

Your imported documents, folder bookmarks, generated records, preferences, and other content are stored locally on your device. We do not have access to this data.

If you enable iCloud sync, your data is stored in your personal iCloud account and is governed by Apple's privacy policy. You can disable iCloud sync at any time in Settings.

When you connect a WebDAV server, your username and password are stored only in the on-device Keychain and are used solely to access the server you configured; they are never uploaded to us.

We do not use any third-party analytics, crash reporting, tracking, or advertising services.

HashRead does not knowingly collect any information from children. The app contains no ads and no links to external sites beyond the Feedback email.

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.

If you have any questions or concerns about privacy, please contact us at kazecreator@gmail.com.
