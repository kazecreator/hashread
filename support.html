常见问题

  • 导入文件、本地文件夹和生成的记录保存在本机；删除 App 可能会删除本机数据。
  • 开启 iCloud 同步后，文库、导入文件和生成记录会自动同步到你的个人 iCloud。
  • WebDAV 文档需同步或打开下载后才能离线阅读；未缓存内容需要网络。
  • 导出功能会把 PDF 或图片写入缓存或保存到相册，应用不会读取你的相册内容。
  • 聊天记录请先通过电脑端工具导出，再使用文件导入。
  • 当前版本不提供账号登录、社区发布或举报功能，也不含广告。

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Imported files, local folders, and generated records are stored on this device. Removing the app may delete local data.
  • With iCloud sync enabled, your library, imported files, and generated records sync automatically to your personal iCloud.
  • WebDAV documents must be synced or opened before they can be read offline; uncached content needs a network connection.
  • Export writes PDFs or images to the cache or saves them to Photos. The app does not read your photo library.
  • Export chat logs on a computer first, then import the file into HashRead.
  • This version does not provide account sign-in, community publishing, or reporting features, and contains no ads.
